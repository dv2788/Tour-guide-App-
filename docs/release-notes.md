# Roamie release notes

## 1.0.0-beta.1 (November 2026)

The first beta: everything a traveller needs to try Roamie for real, free, in the browser.

**Plan**
- "Suggest a route for me": say what you'd like to see (history, culture, landscapes, food), the
  kind of outing (city walk, hike, bike ride, scenic drive), how long (30 minutes to a long day)
  and how far (up to 100 km). Up to three itineraries to choose from.
- Each place gets the time it deserves: a look at a statue, a while at a church, longer at a
  cathedral, a castle, a park or a great museum; more when the day has time to spare.
- Lunch, dinner, coffee or a drink at the right time, at independent places that are open; on
  a food outing, a local taste about every hour.
- Start from where you are, from a typed city, or from the part of town you're staying in.
- Paste a route from Google Maps or any maps app, or type your own stops. 21 sample tours.

**On the way**
- Turn-by-turn directions, spoken before each turn, with real street names, for walking,
  hiking, running, cycling and driving.
- The story of every place you pass, from Wikipedia, in English, French, Spanish, German or
  Italian, with a calm guide or a documentary narrator (voices run on the phone).
- First person: travel through the streets in 3D, facing the way you go, with signposts for the
  stops; with a Google key, real Street View photos instead.
- Point your camera at a building to hear what it is, and be guided there.
- What's on in town: Carnival, Christmas markets, New Year.

**Private and safe**
- No account, no tracking, nothing stored outside the phone; "Forget everything" in one tap.
- A strict security policy on every page; the Street View page isolated in its own frame.

**Known limits**
- Wikipedia is sometimes busy: the app waits and retries, and says so rather than guessing.
- The free routing service can be slow at peak times; directions arrive a few seconds late.
- Street View needs a Google key (off by default); the 3D view shows plain building shapes.
- Roamie Plus (one city free, €9.99 once for all) is built but switched off for the beta.
