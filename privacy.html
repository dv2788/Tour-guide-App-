Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

In short: Roamie has no account, no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and no server of its own that stores anything about you. What you type and where you are stays on your phone, except what has to be sent to the map, route and encyclopedia services below to answer you. The camera picture never leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings and plans: city, part of town, stops, way of getting around, language, voice, range, your last suggested route. Kept in your browser's storage so they're there next time.
  • The app itself and any voice you download, so they work with a weak connection.
  • Your location is used on the phone to guide you and to tell you about places you pass. Roamie keeps no history of where you've been.
  • The camera (in "Point your camera") shows the picture on your screen only. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere: what you're looking at is worked out from your position and compass.

What is sent, and to whom

Like any website, each service below receives your device's internet address (IP address) with each request, and the details listed. Roamie doesn't add your name, an identifier or anything else about you.

Roamie's fonts are served from Roamie itself, not from Google Fonts. Some of these services are outside the European Union; they receive only what's needed for the request. Roamie doesn't sell, share or use your data for anything else.

Permissions

Your phone asks before Roamie may use your location, camera or compass ("motion & orientation"), and you can say no: you can still type a city, plan a route, try a tour from your sofa, and read about places. You can change these permissions at any time in your browser's settings.

Your rights

Roamie holds no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete: use the button above to clear your phone. For what the services above receive, see their privacy policies (linked). If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on Roamie's GitHub page.
