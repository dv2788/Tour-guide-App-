Free audio walking tour · Lima

The City of Kings, from Plaza San Martín to San Francisco

6 stops · 1.9 km on foot · about 1 hour

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

Start this tour Not in Lima? The app can simulate the walk, so you can try it from your sofa.

The stops

  1. I

    Plaza San Martín

    Cultural heritage site in Peru

    San Martín Square is one of the most representative public spaces of the city of Lima, Peru. It is located at the ninth block of Colmena avenue, within the Historic Centre of Lima which was declared a World Heritage Site in 1988 by UNESCO.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Plaza de Armas de Lima

    Cultural heritage site in Peru

    The Plaza Mayor, also known as the Plaza de Armas, is the main public square of the historic centre of the city of Lima, Peru. It dates back to the foundation of the city by Francisco Pizarro on 18 January 1535.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Palacio de Gobierno

    Building in Lima, Peru

    The Government Palace, also known as the House of Pizarro, is the seat of the executive branch of the Peruvian government, and the official residence of the president of Peru.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Catedral de Lima

    Cultural heritage site in Lima, Peru

    The Cathedral Basilica of Lima, commonly known as the Metropolitan Cathedral of Lima, is a Roman Catholic cathedral in Lima, Peru. It is the seat of the Archdiocese of Lima.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Palacio Arzobispal

    Palace in Lima, Peru

    The Archbishop’s Palace of Lima, the capital of Peru, is the residence of the Archbishop of Lima, and the administrative headquarters of the Archdiocese of Lima. It is located at the Plaza Mayor, in the Historic Centre of Lima.

  6. VI

    Convento de San Francisco

    Cultural heritage site in Peru

    The Basilica and Convent of San Francisco is a Catholic church building located in the Historic Centre of Lima, Peru.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Fiestas Patrias, 28 and 29 July. Peru's independence days: a military parade in Lima on the 29th, flags everywhere; some museums close. Check the dates
  • Lord of Miracles, October. Lima's purple month: huge processions of the Señor de los Milagros, the biggest around 18 and 28 October. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
