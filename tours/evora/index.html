The stops

  1. I

    Templo Romano de Évora

    Temple in Évora, Portugal

    The Roman Temple of Évora, also referred to as the Templo de Diana is an ancient temple in the Portuguese city of Évora. The temple is part of the historical centre of the city, which was included in the classification by UNESCO as a World Heritage Site.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Sé de Évora

    Roman Catholic church in Évora, Portugal

    The Cathedral of Évora is a Roman Catholic church in the city of Évora, Portugal. It is one of the oldest and most important local monuments, lying on the highest spot of the city. It is part of the historical city centre, and the seat of the Archdiocese of Évora.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Igreja de São Francisco

    Church building in Portugal

    The Church of St. Francis is located in Évora, Portugal. It is best known for its Chapel of Bones. The Church was classified as National Monument in 1910.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Capela dos Ossos

    Historical monument in Évora, Portugal

    The Capela dos Ossos is a monument in Évora, Portugal. It is a small interior chapel located next to the entrance of the Church of St. Francis. The Chapel gets its name because the interior walls are covered and decorated with human skulls and bones.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Universidade de Évora

    Public university in Évora, Portugal

    The University of Évora is a public university in Évora, Portugal.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
