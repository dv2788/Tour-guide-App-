The Sassi: cave houses, rock churches and the cathedral

8 stops · 2.9 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Palombaro Lungo

    Matera's great cistern under Piazza Vittorio Veneto, dug out of the rock in the 16th century and in use until the 1920s: 15 metres deep, 50 long, it held five million litres of rainwater for a city that had no river.

  2. II

    Sassi di Matera

    UNESCO World Heritage Site in Basilicata, Italy

    The Sassi di Matera are two districts of the Italian city of Matera, Basilicata, well-known for their ancient cave dwellings inhabited since the Paleolithic period.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Cattedrale di Matera

    Cathedral in Matera, Basilicata, Italy

    Matera Cathedral is a Roman Catholic cathedral in Matera, Basilicata, Italy. It is dedicated to the Virgin Mary under the designation of the Madonna della Bruna and to Saint Eustace.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    San Pietro Caveoso

    Catholic church in Matera, Italy

    San Pietro Caveoso, also known as "Saint Peter and Saint Paul Church", is a Catholic church situated in the Sassi of Matera.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Madonna de Idris

    A rock church hollowed into the Monterrone crag above the Sasso Caveoso, with the chapel of San Giovanni in Monterrone behind it and frescoes from the 12th to the 17th century. The view over the ravine of the Gravina is the one on the postcards.

  6. VI

    Casa Grotta di Vico Solitario

    A cave dwelling furnished as a family lived in it until the 1950s: one room for parents, children, the mule and the hens, a cistern under the floor, a manger by the bed. It shows why the Sassi were cleared and why they were later saved.

  7. VII

    Palazzo Lanfranchi

    Museum in Matera, Basilicata, Italy

    The Museo nazionale d'arte medievale e moderna della Basilicata is an art museum located inside the building Palazzo Lanfranchi, which is located in Piazzetta Pascoli, in the Historic Centre of Matera Basilicata, Italy.

  8. VIII

    Convento di Sant'Agostino

    The Augustinian convent of 1592 at the northern edge of the Sasso Barisano, on a spur above the ravine. Its terrace gives the classic view of the old town, and under the church lies the rock chapel of San Giuliano.

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
