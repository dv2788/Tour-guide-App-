Free audio walking tour · Rio de Janeiro

Old Rio, from Candelária to the Selarón Steps

7 stops · 3.4 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

Start this tour Not in Rio de Janeiro? The app can simulate the walk, so you can try it from your sofa.

The stops

  1. I

    Igreja da Candelária

    Roman Catholic church in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

    The Candelária Church is an important historical Roman Catholic church in the city of Rio de Janeiro, in southeastern Brazil. It was built and decorated during a long period, from 1775 to the late 19th century.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Paço Imperial

    Building in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

    The Paço Imperial, or Imperial Palace, previously known as the Royal Palace of Rio de Janeiro and Palace of the Viceroys, is a historic building in the center of the city of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Confeitaria Colombo

    Restaurant in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

    Confeitaria Colombo is a coffeehouse located in downtown Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. It is one of the main landmarks of the city's Central Region. Website City Guides named Colombo one of the 10 most beautiful cafés in the world.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Theatro Municipal

    Opera house in Centro, Rio de Janeiro

    The Theatro Municipal is an opera house in the Centro district of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Built in the early twentieth century, it is considered to be one of the most beautiful and important theaters in the country.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Museu Nacional de Belas Artes

    Fine art museum in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

    The Museu Nacional de Belas Artes is a national art museum located in the city of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The museum, officially established in 1937 by the initiative of education minister Gustavo Capanema, was inaugurated in 1938 by President Getúlio Vargas.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Arcos da Lapa

    Aqueduct in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

    The Carioca Aqueduct, also known as Arcos da Lapa, is an aqueduct in the city of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The aqueduct was built in the middle of the 18th century to bring fresh water from the Carioca River to the population of the city.

    More on Wikipedia
  7. VII

    Escadaria Selarón

    Set of steps in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

    Escadaria Selarón, locally known as the 'Lapa Steps' for being situated at the "Lapa" neighborhood, is a set of world-famous steps in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. They are the work of Chilean-born artist Jorge Selarón who claimed it as "my tribute to the Brazilian people".

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Rio Carnival, the days before Ash Wednesday. Samba school parades in the Sambadrome and street parties (blocos) all over the city. Book well ahead. Check the dates
  • New Year's Eve on Copacabana, 31 December. Millions gather on the beach, many dressed in white, for the fireworks at midnight. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
