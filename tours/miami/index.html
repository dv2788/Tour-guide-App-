Free audio walking tour · Miami Beach

Art Deco South Beach and the Versace mansion

5 stops · 2.9 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Art Deco Welcome Center

    U.S. historic district in Miami Beach, Florida

    S. historic district located in the South Beach neighborhood of Miami Beach, Florida. The area is well known as the district where Italian fashion designer Gianni Versace lived and was assassinated by Andrew Cunanan, in a mansion on Ocean Drive.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Casa Casuarina

    Former home of Gianni Versace

    Casa Casuarina, also known as the Versace Mansion, is an American property built in 1930, renowned for being owned by and the place of the murder of the Italian fashion designer Gianni Versace; he lived there from 1992 until his death in 1997.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    The Wolfsonian–FIU

    Art museum in Florida, United States

    The Wolfsonian–Florida International University or The Wolfsonian–FIU, located in the heart of the Art Deco District of Miami Beach, Florida, is a museum, library and research center with collections intended to illustrate the persuasive power of art and design.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Española Way

    A pedestrian street of 1920s Mediterranean Revival buildings in the heart of South Beach, lined with cafés and restaurants.

  5. V

    Lincoln Road

    United States historic place

    Lincoln Road is a street in Miami Beach, Florida, United States. The most famous section is Lincoln Road Mall, a pedestrian road running east–west parallel between 16th Street and 17th Street.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Art Basel Miami Beach, early December. The art fair takes over the Convention Center, with shows and parties all over town; hotels fill up. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
