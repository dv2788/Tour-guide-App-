Free audio walking tour · Tainan

Taiwan's old capital: Chihkan Tower, the Confucian temple and Koxinga's shrine

6 stops · 2.5 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

Start this tour Not in Tainan? The app can simulate the walk, so you can try it from your sofa.

The stops

  1. I

    Chihkan Tower

    Dutch outpost in Taiwan

    Fort Provintia or Providentia, also known as Chihkan Tower, was a Dutch outpost on Formosa at a site now located in West Central District, Tainan, Taiwan. It was built in 1653 during the Dutch colonization of Taiwan. 2 km) due east from modern-day Anping.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Grand Matsu Temple

    Mazu temple in Tainan, Taiwan

    The Grand Matsu Temple, also known as the Datianhou or Great Queen of Heaven Temple, is a Taiwanese temple to the goddess Mazu, who is the Goddess of Sea and Patron Deity of fishermen, sailors and any occupations related to the sea.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Hayashi Department Store

    Department store in West Central, Tainan, Taiwan

    The Hayashi Department Store is a department store in West Central District, Tainan, Taiwan.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Tainan Confucius Temple

    Temple in West Central, Tainan, Taiwan

    The Tainan Confucian Temple or Quan Tai Shou Xue, is a Confucian temple on Nanmen Road (南門路) in West Central District, Tainan, Taiwan.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Tainan Art Museum

    Museum in West Central, Tainan, Taiwan

    The Tainan Art Museum is an art museum in West Central District, Tainan, Taiwan.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Koxinga Shrine

    Temple in Taiwan, formerly a Shinto Shrine

    Koxinga Shrine is a Taiwanese historical temple originally known as Kaishan Temple. Located in Tainan, Taiwan, it was built in honor of Koxinga.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
