Transylvania's Saxon squares, the Council Tower and the Bridge of Lies

5 stops · 0.6 km on foot · about 1 hour

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Piața Mare

    City square in Sibiu, Romania

    Piața Mare is the central city square in Sibiu, Romania.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Brukenthal National Museum

    Museum in Sibiu, Romania

    The Brukenthal National Museum is a museum in Sibiu, Transylvania, Romania, established in the late 18th century by Samuel von Brukenthal (1721-1803) in his city palace.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Council Tower

    Tower in Sibiu, Romania

    The Council Tower of Sibiu is a tower situated between the two main squares of the Historic center in Sibiu, the Great Square and the Small Square.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Podul Minciunilor

    Bridge in Transylvania, Romania

    The Bridge of Lies is a legendary pedestrian bridge located in the center of the Transylvanian city of Sibiu in central Romania. There are many legends surrounding the bridge because of its name. It is the first cast iron bridge built in what is today Romania.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Lutheran Cathedral of Saint Mary

    Church in Sibiu, Romania

    The Lutheran Cathedral of Saint Mary is the most famous Gothic-style church in Sibiu, Transylvania, central Romania. 34 m high steeple is a landmark of the city.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
