The stops

  1. I

    Jameh Mosque of Yazd

    Shi'ite mosque in Yazd, Yazd province, Iran

    The Jāmeh Mosque of Yazd is a Friday mosque (jāmeh) located in the city of Yazd, in the province of Yazd, Iran. Built on the site of a 9th-century CE fire temple, the mosque was completed during the 14th and 15th centuries CE and renovated in subsequent years.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Alexander's Prison

    A 15th-century domed school with a deep round pit in its courtyard, said to be the dungeon Alexander the Great built and Hafez wrote of in a poem. Nowadays a small museum of the old quarter, next to the Tomb of the Twelve Imams.

  3. III

    Yazd Water Museum

    A merchant's house of 1929 built over two qanats, the underground channels that bring mountain water across the desert. Its rooms explain how the channels were dug and kept, and you can go down to the water itself.

  4. IV

    Amir Chakhmaq Complex

    Caravanserai and square complex in Yazd, Iran

    The Amir Chakhmaq Complex is a prominent structure in Yazd, Iran, noted for its symmetrical sunken alcoves. It is a mosque located on a square of the same name. It also contains a caravanserai, a tekyeh, a bathhouse, a cold water well, and a confectionery.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Zoroastrian Fire Temple

    Zoroastrian fire temple in Yazd, Iran

    The Fire Temple of Yazd, also known as Yazd Atash Behram, is a Zoroastrian fire temple in Yazd, Yazd province, Iran. It enshrines the Atash Behram, meaning "Victorious Fire", dated to 470 AD.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Dowlat Abad Garden

    UNESCO World Heritage Site in Iran

    Dowlatabad Garden is a historical garden in Yazd, Iran. 8 meters tall windcatcher is the tallest adobe-made windcatcher in the world. It was listed as a UNESCO world heritage site in 2011, as part of the Persian gardens.

    More on Wikipedia
