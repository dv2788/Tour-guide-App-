The stops

  1. I

    Daereungwon

    Tomb complex in Gyeongju, South Korea

    Daereungwon is a complex of Silla-era tumulus tombs in Gyeongju, South Korea. Since 2011, it has been a designated Historic Site. Daereungwon is a popular tourist attraction. In 2023, it was reported that it received around one million visitors on average per year.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Cheomseongdae

    Observatory in Gyeongju, South Korea

    Cheomseongdae is an astronomical observatory in Gyeongju, South Korea. Cheomseongdae is the oldest surviving astronomical observatory in Asia, and possibly even the world. It was constructed during the Silla period, during the reign of Queen Seondeok.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Gyochon Traditional Village

    Folk village in Gyeongju, South Korea

    Gyochon Traditional Village (Korean: 교촌마을) is a Korean folk village in Gyeongju, South Korea.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Woljeonggyo Bridge

    Bridge in Gyeongju, South Korea

    Woljeonggyo is a covered bridge in Gyeongju, South Korea. D. during the Unified Silla period and lost during the Joseon period, it was rebuilt and opened in April 2018.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond

    Palace and pond in Gyeongju, South Korea

    Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond are a Silla-era former palace and artificial pond in Gyeongju, South Korea. They were part of the palace complex of ancient Silla. It was constructed by order of King Munmu in 674 CE.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Gyeongju National Museum

    National museum in South Korea

    The Gyeongju National Museum (Korean: 국립경주박물관) is a museum in Gyeongju, South Korea. Its holdings are largely devoted to relics of the Silla kingdom, of which Gyeongju was the capital.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
