The stops

  1. I

    Kaminarimon

    Gate entrance in Asakusa, Japan

    The Kaminarimon is the outer of two large entrance gates that ultimately leads to the Sensō-ji in Asakusa, Tokyo, Japan. The gate, with its lantern and statues, is popular with tourists. 3 m2.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Sensō-ji

    Buddhist temple in Tokyo, Japan

    Sensō-ji , is an ancient Buddhist temple in Asakusa, Tokyo, Japan. It is Tokyo's oldest-established temple, and one of its most significant. It is dedicated to Kannon, the bodhisattva of compassion.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Asakusa Shrine

    Shinto shrine in Asakusa district, Tokyo, Japan

    Asakusa Shrine is a Shinto shrine in the Asakusa district of Tokyo, Japan. Also known as Sanja-sama , it is one of the most famous Shinto shrines in the city.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Sumida Park

    Public park in Tokyo, Japan

    Sumida Park is a public park in Sumida and Taitō, Tokyo, Japan. Cherry blossoms can be seen in spring, and the Sumidagawa Fireworks Festival is held in July.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Cherry blossom season, late March to early April (it varies with the weather). Picnics under the blossom in the parks; the most famous spots are very crowded at weekends. Check the dates
  • Japanese New Year, 1 to 3 January. Shrines and temples are packed for the first visit of the year; many shops and museums close. Check the dates
  • Sumidagawa Fireworks Festival, the last Saturday of July. Twenty thousand fireworks over the Sumida River near Asakusa; the area is packed from the afternoon. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
