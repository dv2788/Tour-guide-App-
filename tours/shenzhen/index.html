ROAMIE

Free audio walking tour · Shenzhen

Lotus Hill to the Ping An tower, along Futian’s axis

5 stops · 4 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

Start this tour Not in Shenzhen? The app can simulate the walk, so you can try it from your sofa.

The stops

  1. I

    Lianhuashan Park

    Public park in Shenzhen, China

    Lianhuashan Park is a hill and urban park in Shenzhen, China, inaugurated in 1997. It is located at the northern end of the Futian Central Business District and covers an area of 150 hectares. It is within walking distance from Children's Palace Station of the Shenzhen Metro.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Shenzhen Library

    Municipal public library in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China

    Shenzhen Library is the municipal public library of Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. The main library is a part of the Shenzhen Cultural Center in Futian.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Shenzhen Civic Center

    Government building in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China

    Civic Center or Citizens' Center is a government building in Shenzhen, China. 172 billion. The civic building was the centerpiece plan for the Shenzhen Futian Central District Master Plan and is "attentive to traditional asian themes and traditions".

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Shenzhen Museum

    Museum in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China

    Shenzhen Museum is a public municipal museum in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. It is funded by the Shenzhen Municipal People's Government.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Ping An Finance Center

    Supertall skyscraper in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China

    1-metre (1,966-foot) supertall skyscraper in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. It was completed in 2017, and is the tallest building in Shenzhen, the 2nd tallest building in China, and the 5th tallest building in the world.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
