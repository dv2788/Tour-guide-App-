The stops

  1. I

    Casa Rosada

    Office building of the President of Argentina in the City of Buenos Aires

    The Casa Rosada, lit. 'the pink house', is the president of the Argentine Republic's official workplace, located in Buenos Aires. The palatial mansion is known officially as Casa de Gobierno.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Catedral Metropolitana de Buenos Aires

    Cathedral in Argentina

    The Metropolitan Cathedral of the Most Holy Trinity of Buenos Aires is a Roman Catholic cathedral in Buenos Aires, the capital city of Argentina.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Plaza de Mayo

    Public square in Buenos Aires, Argentina

    The Plaza de Mayo is the main city square of Buenos Aires, Argentina. It was formed in 1884 after the demolition of the Recova building, unifying the city's Plaza Mayor and Plaza de Armas, by that time known as Plaza de la Victoria and Plaza 25 de Mayo, respectively.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Cabildo de Buenos Aires

    Historic museum in Buenos Aires, Argentina

    The Cabildo of Buenos Aires is the public building in the city of Buenos Aires, Argentina, that was used as a seat of the town council during the colonial era and the government house of the Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Palacio Barolo

    Office building in Buenos Aires, Argentina

    Palacio Barolo is a landmark office building, located at 1370 Avenida de Mayo, in the neighborhood of Monserrat, Buenos Aires, Argentina. It stood as Buenos Aires' tallest building for more than a decade until the construction of the Kavanagh Building in 1936.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Obelisco de Buenos Aires

    Architectural structure

    The Obelisco de Buenos Aires is a national historic monument and icon of Buenos Aires.

    More on Wikipedia
  7. VII

    Teatro Colón

    Opera house in Buenos Aires

    The Teatro Colón is a historic opera house in Buenos Aires, Argentina. It is considered one of the ten best opera houses in the world by National Geographic.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Tango Buenos Aires Festival, late August to early September. Free concerts, dance classes and the Tango World Championship, with milongas all over the city. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
