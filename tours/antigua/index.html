Free audio walking tour · Antigua Guatemala

Baroque ruins and the Santa Catalina arch under the volcanoes

5 stops · 1.7 km on foot · about 1 hour

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Catedral de San José

    Cathedral in Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala

    Parish of San José, located in the city of Antigua Guatemala, is part of the Archdiocese of Santiago de Guatemala and is located in a section of the old Primate Cathedral of Antigua Guatemala, which was destroyed by the 1773 Guatemala earthquakes.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Palacio de los Capitanes Generales

    Building in Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala

    The Captain General Palace, or Palacio de los Capitanes Generales, is a large building localed in the Central Square of Antigua Guatemala.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Arco de Santa Catalina

    Monument in Antigua Guatemala

    The Santa Catalina Arch is one of the distinguishable landmarks in Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala, located on 5th Avenue North.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    La Merced

    Building in Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala

    The Church and Convent of La Merced is a Catholic church located in the city of Antigua Guatemala in Guatemala. The architect Juan de Dios Estrada was in charge of its construction from 1749. The church was dedicated in 1767.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Convento de las Capuchinas

    Building in Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala

    The Iglesia y Convento de las Capuchinas is a notable convent and church in Antigua Guatemala, Guatemala. It is one of the finest examples of an 18th-century convent in Guatemala.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
