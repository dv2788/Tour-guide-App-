The stops

  1. I

    Mdina Gate

    City gate in Mdina, Malta

    Mdina Gate, also known as the Main Gate or the Vilhena Gate, is the main gate into the fortified city of Mdina, Malta. It was built in the Baroque style in 1724 to designs of Charles François de Mondion, during the magistracy of Grand Master António Manoel de Vilhena.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    St. Paul's Cathedral

    Church in Mdina, Malta

    The Metropolitan Cathedral of Saint Paul, commonly known as St Paul's Cathedral or the Mdina Cathedral, is a Catholic cathedral in Mdina, Malta, dedicated to St. Paul the Apostle.

  3. III

    Palazzo Falson

    Residence used as a historic house museum in Mdina, Malta

    Palazzo Falson, formerly known as Palazzo Cumbo-Navarra, Casa dei Castelletti, and the Norman House, is a medieval townhouse in Mdina, Malta. It was built as a family residence by the Maltese nobility, and is named after the Falson family.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Carmelite Priory

    Church in Mdina, Malta

    The Church of the Annunciation also known as The Carmelite Church is a rich Baroque priory church of the Priory of Our Lady of Mount Carmel located in Mdina, Malta.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Domus Romana

    Roman and Muslim archaeological site in Malta

    The Domus Romana, stylized as the Domvs Romana, is a ruined Roman-era house located on the boundary between Mdina and Rabat, Malta. It was built in the 1st century BC as an aristocratic town house (domus) within the Roman city of Melite.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    St. Paul's Catacombs

    Christian catacombs in Malta

    St. Paul's Catacombs are some of the most prominent features of Malta's early Christianity archeology. The archeological clearing of the site has revealed an extensive system of underground galleries and tombs dating from the third to the eighth centuries CE.

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
