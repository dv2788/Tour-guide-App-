Ortigia: the Temple of Apollo, the Duomo and the Fountain of Arethusa

5 stops · 1.6 km on foot · about 1 hour

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Tempio di Apollo

    Ancient Greek monument in Syracuse, Sicily, Italy

    The Temple of Apollo is one of the most important ancient Greek monuments of Magna Graecia on Ortygia, in front of the Piazza Pancali in Syracuse, Sicily, Italy.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Antico Mercato di Ortigia

    The old market hall of 1900 by the Temple of Apollo and the street market around it, where Ortigia still buys its fish, cheese, oranges and almonds every morning but Sunday.

  3. III

    Cathedral of Syracuse

    UNESCO World Heritage Site

    The Cathedral of Syracuse, formally the Cattedrale metropolitana della Natività di Maria Santissima, is an ancient Catholic church in Syracuse, Sicily, the seat of the Catholic Archdiocese of Siracusa.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Fountain of Arethusa

    Sicilian natural fountain that features in mythology and literature

    The Fountain of Arethusa is a natural spring on the island of Ortygia in the historical centre of the city of Syracuse in Sicily.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Castello Maniace

    Building in Syracuse, Italy

    The Castello Maniace is a citadel and castle in Syracuse, Sicily, southern Italy. It is situated at the far point of the Ortygia island promontory, where it was constructed between 1232 and 1240 by the Emperor Frederick II.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
