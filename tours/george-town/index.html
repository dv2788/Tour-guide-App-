Free audio walking tour · George Town

Clan houses, the Kapitan Keling mosque and the Blue Mansion

7 stops · 5.3 km on foot · about 2 hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Fort Cornwallis

    Bastion fort in George Town, Penang, Malaysia

    Fort Cornwallis is a bastion fort in George Town, Penang, Malaysia, built by the British East India Company in the late 18th century.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Goddess of Mercy Temple

    Taoist temple in George Town, Malaysia

    The Goddess of Mercy Temple, also known as Kuan Im Teng or Kong Hock Keong, is a Mahayana Buddhist temple within George Town in the Malaysian state of Penang. Located at Pitt Street, it was built in 1800, making it the oldest Chinese temple in the state.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Kapitan Keling Mosque

    Mosque in George Town, Penang, Malaysia

    The Kapitan Keling Mosque is a mosque built in the 19th century by Indian Muslim traders in George Town, Penang, Malaysia. It is situated on the corner of Buckingham Street and Pitt Street.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Khoo Kongsi

    Hokkien clan temple in Penang, Malaysia

    The Leong San Tong Khoo Kongsi, or Khoo Kongsi for short, is the largest Hokkien clanhouse in Malaysia, with elaborate and highly ornamented architecture, a mark of the dominant presence of the Chinese in Penang, Malaysia.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Pinang Peranakan Mansion

    Historic house museum in Penang, Malaysia

    The Pinang Peranakan Mansion in George Town, Penang, Malaysia, is a museum dedicated to Penang's Peranakan heritage.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Cheong Fatt Tze Mansion

    Boutique hotel in Penang, Malaysia

    The Cheong Fatt Tze Mansion is a government gazetted heritage building located on Leith Street in George Town, Penang, Malaysia.

    More on Wikipedia
  7. VII

    Clan Jetties

    Neighbourhood of George Town, Penang, Malaysia

    The Clan Jetties form a residential neighbourhood within the city of George Town in the Malaysian state of Penang.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
