Free audio walking tour · Mostar

The Old Bridge, the bazaar and the mosques of the old town

5 stops · 2 km on foot · about 1 hour

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Stari Most

    Bridge in Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina

    The Old Bridge, also known as the Mostar Bridge, is a rebuilt 16th-century Ottoman bridge in the city of Mostar in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Koski Mehmed Pasha Mosque

    Built in 1617 on a terrace above the Neretva, Mostar's best-known mosque after the bridge: a single dome, a slender minaret you can climb, and from its courtyard the view of the Old Bridge that every photograph of the city is taken from.

  3. III

    Crooked Bridge

    Bridge in Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina

    Kriva Ćuprija is a small stone bridge across the Radobolja river in Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina. The bridge is oldest one arch bridge in Mostar, built in 1558 during the Ottoman rule. Its builder was the Ottoman architect Ćejvan Ketoda.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Karađoz-begova džamija

    Mosque in Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina

    The Karađoz Bey Mosque is a Sunni mosque, located in the city of Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina. Completed in 965 AH (1557/1558 CE) during the Ottoman era, the mosque was destroyed in the early 1990s during the Bosnian War, and rebuilt in 2004.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Muslibegović House

    Traditional residential in Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina

    Muslibegović House is a national monument by KONS, and a museum located in Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina. The monument complex includes a residential building and two courtyards, including the surrounding walls with entrance gateways, and movable property.

    More on Wikipedia

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
