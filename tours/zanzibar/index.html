The stops

  1. I

    Darajani Market

    The Darajani Market is the main bazaar in Stone Town, Zanzibar. It is also known as Estella Market and informally as Marikiti Kuu. The market is located in Darajani Road, in the surroundings of the Anglican Cathedral of Christ.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Christ Church

    Anglican cathedral in Stone Town, Zanzibar, Tanzania

    Christ Church is an Anglican cathedral in Stone Town, Zanzibar, Tanzania. It belongs to the Anglican Church of Tanzania. It is a landmark historical church, as well as one of the most prominent examples of early Christian architecture in East Africa.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Old Dispensary

    Historic building in Stone Town, Zanzibar

    The Old Dispensary, also known as Ithnashiri Dispensary, is a historical building in Stone Town, Zanzibar. It is located on the seafront, in Mizingani Road, halfway between the Palace Museum and the harbour.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Palace Museum

    Museum in Zanzibar

    The Sultan's Palace is one of the main historical buildings of Stone Town, Zanzibar, Tanzania. It is a 3-story building with merlon-decorated white walls, located in Mizingani Road, on the seafront, between the House of Wonders and the Old Dispensary.

  5. V

    House of Wonders

    Landmark building in Stone Town, Zanzibar

    The House of Wonders or Palace of Wonders is a landmark building in Stone Town, Zanzibar. It is the largest and tallest building of Stone Town and occupies a prominent place facing the Forodhani Gardens on the old town's seafront in Mizingani Road.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Old Fort

    Fort in Zanzibar, Tanzania

    The Old Fort, also known as the Arab Fort and by other names, is a fortification located in Stone Town, the capital of Zanzibar. It is the oldest building and a major visitor attraction of Stone Town.

    More on Wikipedia
  7. VII

    Forodhani Park

    Park in Zanzibar, Tanzania

    The Forodhani Gardens is a small park in the historical city of Stone Town, Zanzibar, Tanzania. , the House of Wonders and the Old Fort.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Ramadan, a month that moves about 11 days earlier each year (the dates depend on the moon). Many restaurants close by day and opening hours are shorter; after sunset the streets come alive. Be discreet about eating and drinking in public during the day. Check the dates
  • Eid al-Fitr, the end of Ramadan (the dates depend on the moon). A family holiday: some shops, souks and sites close for a day or two. Check the dates
  • Eid al-Adha, about 70 days after Ramadan (the dates depend on the moon). The biggest holiday of the year (Tabaski in Senegal): many shops and some sites close for a few days. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
