Free audio walking tour · Santiago

La Moneda, the Plaza de Armas and Santa Lucía Hill

7 stops · 3.4 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

Start this tour Not in Santiago? The app can simulate the walk, so you can try it from your sofa.

The stops

  1. I

    Palacio de La Moneda

    Building in Santiago, Chile

    La Moneda Palace, or simply La Moneda, is the seat of the president of the Republic of Chile. It also houses the offices of three cabinet ministers: Interior, General Secretariat of the Presidency, and General Secretariat of the Government.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Plaza de la Constitución

    Town square in Santiago, Chile

    The Plaza de la Constitución is a plaza occupying a full square block in the heart of the civic district of Santiago, Chile.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Museo de Arte Precolombino

    Art museum in Santiago, Chile

    The Chilean Museum of Pre-Columbian Art is an art museum dedicated to the study and display of pre-Columbian artworks and artifacts from Central and South America.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Plaza de Armas

    Main square of Santiago, Chile

    The Plaza de Armas is the main square of Santiago, the capital of Chile. Plaza de Armas metro station is located under the square.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Catedral Metropolitana de Santiago de Chile

    Roman Catholic Cathedral in Chile

    Santiago Metropolitan Cathedral is the seat of the Archdiocese of Santiago and the main temple of the Catholic Church in that country. As a Cathedral Church, it is the permanent seat of the Archbishop of Santiago and is dedicated to the Assumption of the Virgin Mary.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Mercado Central

    Market in Chile

    The Mercado Central de Santiago is the central market of Santiago de Chile. It was opened in 1872 and Fermín Vivaceta was in charge of its construction. The market replaced the Plaza del Abasto, which was destroyed by a fire in 1864.

    More on Wikipedia
  7. VII

    Cerro Santa Lucía

    Hill in Santiago, Chile

    Santa Lucía Hill, also known in Mapuche as Huelén Hill, is a hill in the centre of Santiago, Chile. It is situated between Alameda del Libertador Bernardo O'Higgins in the south, Santa Lucía Street in the west and Victoria Subercaseaux on the east.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Fiestas Patrias, 18 and 19 September. Chile's national days: fondas (fairground parties) with food, cueca dancing and a military parade on the 19th. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
