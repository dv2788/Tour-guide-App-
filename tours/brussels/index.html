Festivals and holidays

  • Fête de la Musique, 21 June. Free concerts in the streets and squares, all evening long. Check the dates
  • European Heritage Days, the third weekend of September. Buildings that are usually closed, ministries and palaces among them, open their doors, mostly for free. Expect queues. Check the dates
  • Belgian National Day, 21 July. Military parade near the Royal Palace and festivities in the park; museums are often free. Check the dates
  • Ommegang, the first week of July. A Renaissance procession on the Grand-Place, with costumes, horses and flag-throwers (tickets for the seats). Check the dates
  • Flower Carpet, a few days around 15 August, every other year. Half a million begonias cover the Grand-Place; the view from the Town Hall balcony is worth the queue. Check the dates
  • Christmas markets, from late November until just before Christmas. Christmas markets in the squares, with mulled wine and crafts. Busiest in the evenings and at weekends. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
