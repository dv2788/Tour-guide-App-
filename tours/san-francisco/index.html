Free audio walking tour · San Francisco

California: the Ferry Building up to Coit Tower

5 stops · 3.7 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

Start this tour Not in San Francisco? The app can simulate the walk, so you can try it from your sofa.

The stops

  1. I

    Ferry Building

    Ferry terminal in San Francisco, California

    The San Francisco Ferry Building is a terminal for ferries that travel across the San Francisco Bay, a food hall and an office building. It is located on The Embarcadero in San Francisco, California, and is served by Golden Gate Ferry and San Francisco Bay Ferry routes.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Transamerica Pyramid

    Skyscraper in San Francisco

    The Transamerica Pyramid Center is a pyramid-shaped 48-story modernist skyscraper in San Francisco, California, United States, and the second tallest building in the San Francisco skyline.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Dragon Gate

    Ceremonial gateway in Chinatown, San Francisco, California, U.S.

    S. state of California. Built in 1969 as a gift from the Republic of China (Taiwan) in the style of a traditional Chinese pailou, it became one of the most photographed locations in Chinatown, along with the older Sing Fat and Sing Chong buildings.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Washington Square

    Park in the North Beach district of San Francisco, California

    1 ha) park in the North Beach district of San Francisco. It was established in 1847 and is one of the city's first parks.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Coit Tower

    United States historic place

    Coit Tower is a 210-foot (64 m) tower in the Telegraph Hill neighborhood of San Francisco, California, overlooking the city and San Francisco Bay.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • San Francisco Pride, the last weekend of June. The parade goes down Market Street on Sunday, with a celebration around Civic Center. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
