Fes el-Bali, from the Blue Gate to the tanneries

6 stops · 1.6 km on foot · about 1 hour

The stops

  1. I

    Bab Bou Jeloud

    City gate in Fez, Morocco

    Bab Bou Jeloud is an ornate city gate in Fes el Bali, the old city of Fez, Morocco. The current gate was built by the French colonial administration in 1913 to serve as the grand entrance to the old city.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Bou Inania Madrasa

    Madrasa, mosque in Fez, Morocco

    The Bou Inania Madrasa or Bu 'Inaniya Madrasa is a madrasa in Fez, Morocco, built in 1350–55 by the Marinid sultan Abu Inan Faris. It is the only madrasa in Morocco which also functioned as a congregational mosque.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Funduq al-Najjarin

    Historical building and museum in Fez, Morocco

    Funduq al-Najjarin is a historic funduq in Fes el Bali, the old medina quarter in the city of Fez, Morocco.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Al-Attarine Madrasa

    Historic madrasa in Fez, Morocco

    The Al-Attarine Madrasa or Medersa al-Attarine is a madrasa in Fez, Morocco, near the Al-Qarawiyyin Mosque. It was built by the Marinid sultan Uthman II Abu Said between 1323 and 1325. The madrasa takes its name from the Souk al-Attarine, the spice and perfume market.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Al-Qarawiyyin Mosque

    University in Fez, Morocco

    The University of al-Qarawiyyin, also written Al-Karaouine or Al Quaraouiyine, is a university located in Fez, Morocco.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Chouara Tannery

    Moroccan tannery in Fez

    Chouara Tannery is one of the three tanneries in the city of Fez, Morocco. It is the largest tannery in the city and one of the oldest. It is located in Fes el Bali, the oldest medina quarter of the city, near the Saffarin Madrasa along the Oued Fes.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Ramadan, a month that moves about 11 days earlier each year (the dates depend on the moon). Many restaurants close by day and opening hours are shorter; after sunset the streets come alive. Be discreet about eating and drinking in public during the day. Check the dates
  • Eid al-Fitr, the end of Ramadan (the dates depend on the moon). A family holiday: some shops, souks and sites close for a day or two. Check the dates
  • Eid al-Adha, about 70 days after Ramadan (the dates depend on the moon). The biggest holiday of the year (Tabaski in Senegal): many shops and some sites close for a few days. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
