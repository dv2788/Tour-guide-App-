Inca walls and colonial churches around the Plaza de Armas

5 stops · 1.3 km on foot · about 1 hour

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Coricancha

    Archaeological site in Peru

    The Coricancha was the most important temple in the Inca Empire, and was described by early Spanish colonialists. It is located in Cusco, Peru, which was the capital of the empire.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Plaza de Armas

    Square in Cusco, Peru

    The Plaza de Armas of Cusco is located in the city of Cusco, Peru. Located in the historic center of the city is the main public space of the town since before its Spanish foundation in 1534.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Catedral del Cusco

    Cathedral in Peru

    The Cathedral of Cusco or Cathedral Basilica of the Virgin of the Assumption is the main temple of the city of Cusco, in Peru and houses the headquarters of the Archdiocese of Cusco.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Iglesia de la Compañía

    Church in Cusco, Peru

    The Iglesia de la Compañía de Jesús is a historic Jesuit church in Cusco, the ancient capital of the Inca Empire, in Cusco Region, Peru. It is situated in the Plaza de Armas de Cusco, the city center. It is built on an Inca palace.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    Twelve-angled Stone

    Incan archaeological artifact in Peru

    The twelve-angled stone is an archeological artifact in Cusco, Peru. It was part of a stone wall of an Inca palace, and is considered to be a national heritage object. The stone is currently part of a wall in the palace of the Archbishop of Cusco.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Inti Raymi, 24 June. The Inca festival of the sun, staged at Qorikancha, the Plaza de Armas and Sacsayhuamán. June is festival month in Cusco. Check the dates
  • Corpus Christi in Cusco, nine weeks after Easter (a Thursday). The saints of the city's churches are carried in procession to the cathedral. Check the dates
  • Fiestas Patrias, 28 and 29 July. Peru's independence days: a military parade in Lima on the 29th, flags everywhere; some museums close. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
