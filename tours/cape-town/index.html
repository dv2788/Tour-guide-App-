The Castle, the old city and the Bo-Kaap

6 stops · 2.9 km on foot · about 1½ hours

Put your phone in your pocket: Roamie shows you the way to each stop and tells you its story as you get there. Free, in your browser, nothing to install.

The stops

  1. I

    Castle of Good Hope

    17th-century bastion fort in Cape Town, South African

    The Castle of Good Hope is a 17th century pentagonal bastion fort in Cape Town, South Africa. Originally located on the coastline of Table Bay, following land reclamation in the Foreshore area, the fort is now located inland. The Castle is surrounded by a canal.

    More on Wikipedia
  2. II

    Cape Town City Hall

    Multi-use hall in Cape Town, South Africa

    Cape Town City Hall is a large Edwardian building, built in 1905, and located in Cape Town's city centre. It is located on the Grand Parade, to the west of the Castle, and is built from honey-coloured oolitic limestone, imported from Bath in England.

    More on Wikipedia
  3. III

    Greenmarket Square

    Historical square in old Cape Town, South Africa

    Greenmarket Square is a historical square in the centre of old Cape Town, South Africa. The square was built in 1696, when a burgher watch house was erected.

    More on Wikipedia
  4. IV

    Bo-Kaap Museum

    Residential area of Cape Town on the slopes of Signal Hill

    The Bo-Kaap is an area of Cape Town, South Africa, formerly known as the Malay Quarter. It is a former racially segregated area, situated on the slopes of Signal Hill above the city centre, and is a historical centre of Cape Malay culture in Cape Town.

    More on Wikipedia
  5. V

    St. George's Cathedral

    Church in Cape Town, South Africa

    St George's Cathedral is the Anglican cathedral in Cape Town, South Africa, and the seat of the Archbishop of Cape Town. St. George's Cathedral is both the metropolitical church of the Anglican Church of Southern Africa and a congregation in the Diocese of Cape Town.

    More on Wikipedia
  6. VI

    Company's Garden

    Park and heritage site in Cape Town, South Africa

    The Company's Garden is a large public park situated in Cape Town CBD - the main commercial district of Cape Town. It is the oldest garden in South Africa, and a national heritage site.

    More on Wikipedia

Festivals and holidays

  • Cape Town Minstrel Carnival, 2 January. Troupes in bright costumes parade through the city centre, singing and playing banjos. Check the dates

How it works

  • Open the tour on your phone and tap Begin the tour. Allow location so Roamie knows where you are.
  • A gold line on the map and a short spoken direction before each turn take you from stop to stop.
  • As you reach each place, and the sights you pass on the way, a calm voice tells you its story. Choose a soothing guide or a documentary narrator, in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian.
