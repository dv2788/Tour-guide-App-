Free, with nothing hidden

Roamie is free: no purchases, no subscription, no ads and no fees, so there is nothing to pay or refund. The optional AI voices are a one-time download of about 80 MB each; on mobile data your operator may charge for it, so use Wi-Fi if you can. Your phone's own voice needs no download.

Stay safe

  • Watch where you're going, not the screen. Roamie is made to be listened to, with the phone in your pocket.
  • On a bike or in a car, set the tour up before you leave and don't touch the phone while moving. Follow the rules of the road and road signs over any direction from Roamie, and only use it where the law allows.
  • Directions can be wrong: maps can be out of date, paths can be closed, and GPS can be off by tens of metres. Use your judgement; never go somewhere unsafe, private or closed because a route goes there.
  • "Point your camera" is for when you're standing still, somewhere safe.

Information, not a guarantee

Stories come from Wikipedia, maps and places from OpenStreetMap, and opening hours from what volunteers have recorded. They are usually right, but can be wrong or out of date. Check what matters (opening hours, prices, access, accessibility) with the place itself. Suggested routes, durations and breaks are estimates.
