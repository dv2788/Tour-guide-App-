    How do I copy my route?
    • Google Maps: plan your route (add stops if you like), tap Share, then Copy.
    • Apple Maps: plan your route, tap the share button, then Copy.
    • Waze: open your destination, tap Share, then Copy.
    • On a computer: copy the address from the browser's address bar.

    OpenStreetMap, Bing and HERE links work too, and so do coordinates and plain lists ("Eiffel Tower → Louvre"). The travel mode is picked up from the link when it has one.
