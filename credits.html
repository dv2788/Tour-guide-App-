Credits

Roamie is built on open data and open software made by thousands of people. Thank you.

Maps, places and routes

  • Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Spotted a mistake on the map? Fix the map.
  • Map by OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles.
  • Routes and street names: the routing service of FOSSGIS e.V. (OSRM), on OpenStreetMap data.
  • Finding places: Nominatim, run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
  • Cafés and restaurants: the Overpass API, on OpenStreetMap data.

Stories and photos

  • Stories are from Wikipedia, under CC BY-SA 4.0; the app links each story to its article, where you'll find its authors. Read aloud, they are shortened.
  • Photos are from Wikimedia Commons, each under its own licence; the app links each photo to its page, which names its author and licence.
  • Place links from Wikidata (CC0).

Voices

The AI voices you can download are Piper voices (MIT licence), run on your phone with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and the eSpeak NG pronunciation engine (GPL-3.0), via piper-wasm. Each voice comes from recordings that people made available:
